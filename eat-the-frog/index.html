Why eat the frog?

Eat The Frog is built on one of the most battle-tested ideas in personal productivity: do your hardest, most important task first, before anything else. The name comes from a line often attributed to Mark Twain — "if it's your job to eat a frog, it's best to do it first thing in the morning" — and was popularised as a productivity method by Brian Tracy in his book Eat That Frog!

Each day you set a single frog — one task that represents the most valuable use of your time. Not a list, not a project board. Get a gentle reminder in the morning, and a real deadline in the evening.

Already using Google Tasks or Microsoft To Do? Connect either one and pick your frog straight from your real task list — completing it in one place completes it in the other.

Features

🐸

One frog a day

No lists, no projects — just the single task that matters most, front and centre every morning.

⏰

Morning reminders

A gentle nudge at your reminder time, with snooze if you need a little longer.

⏳

A real evening deadline

If your frog isn't eaten by your cutoff time, it's marked missed and your streak resets — no cheating.

📅

Today's & Tomorrow's Frog

Line up tomorrow's frog whenever you like — it's always there waiting, not just a once-a-day prompt.

🔥

Streaks

Track your longest run of consecutive eaten frogs, and watch the streak grow.

📋

Achieved & Missed history

A quiet, collapsible record of every frog you've eaten — and the odd one you haven't.

🔄

Google Tasks & Microsoft To Do sync

Pick your frog straight from your existing task list. Complete it in either app and it's marked done in both.

Built to stick

Eat The Frog was built to make one simple productivity trick effortless to actually stick to.
