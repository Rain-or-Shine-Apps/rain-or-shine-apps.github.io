Benefits

🧱

Grows with your child

Starts as a simple calls-and-texts phone at 8, unlocking more as they earn your trust through their teens.

🌿

A real digital detox

Not just for kids — use it on yourself for a genuine break from the apps you can't put down.

🗓️

Schedules that run themselves

Click into "School," "Bedtime," or any mode automatically, once it's set up.

💰

Cheaper than a brick phone

Use the smartphone already gathering dust — no separate device or contract, and it lasts longer.

🔐

Hardened against savvy kids

Real OS-level blocking, not just a closed app they can find a way round.

🔑

You hold the keys

A parent PIN backed by a second factor — recovery codes mean you're never locked out for good.
