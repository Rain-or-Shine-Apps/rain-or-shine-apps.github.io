The Christmas gift that grows with them: a phone that changes as they do

Most phones are the same phone on day one and day 1,000. This one isn't.

13 October 2026

← Back to Blog

With Brickify, the phone you've decided to give them this Christmas can be right for day one, and grow with them as they grow.

Basic brick phones have a shelf life. As soon as homework goes online and friends are in group chats, you'll need a new device. With Brickify, you unbrick things as your child grows. The steps are yours, and Brickify just holds them. All you need to do is change the Brickify profiles. No new device at every stage.

What the steps might look like

Here's one example. The steps, what's in each one, and when you move up are all yours to decide.

  • Starter: calls and texts, maps for the walk to school, and music.
  • Weekend chat: a chat app opens at weekends, as well as everything in Starter.
  • Evenings: a little more on weekday evenings, once homework's done.
  • Nearly there: most apps, most of the time. Bedtime is still calls only.

Each step is just a change to a profile, made with your PIN and second factor. The phone stays the same.
