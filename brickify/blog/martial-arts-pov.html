I do martial arts, this is how I use Brickify

Discipline in the dojo. Discipline on the phone. Same idea.

13 October 2026

← Back to Blog

"My sensei says focus is a muscle. Before grading, my phone does the practice videos Dad saved for me, and a timer."

"After grading, I earned one more app."

How the week is set up

  • Everyday (default): calls, texts, music, camera and Photos, plus any app earned at grading. Runs whenever nothing else is scheduled.
  • School day: Mon to Fri, 08:30 to 15:30: calls and texts only.
  • Dojo nights: Tue and Thu, 17:30 to 20:00: calls, Photos (the downloaded kata videos) and a timer.
  • Grading week: added for that week only, Mon, Wed and Fri, 18:00 to 20:30: calls, Photos and a timer. Delete it after the grading.
  • Bedtime: every night, 21:00 to 07:00: calls only.

How the profiles work: there's one default profile, which the phone falls back to whenever nothing else is scheduled. The other profiles run on their own schedules, and there's always an overnight one. The schedules don't overlap, so it's always clear which profile is running. Emergency calls always work, whatever profile is active.

Good to know

Brickify blocks or allows whole apps. If the kata videos are on a video platform, allowing it opens the whole app, not just those videos. Downloading the clips to Photos keeps it to just the practice videos.
