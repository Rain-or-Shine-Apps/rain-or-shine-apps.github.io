Install Brickify and buy it once

“£4.99. Once. That's the last payment.”

15 October 2026 · about 5 mins

← Back to Blog

Today Brickify goes on. It installs on your child's phone, not yours, because that's the phone it's going to manage.

Do it

  1. On your child's phone, open the App Store or Google Play and install Brickify.
  2. Open it and tap Buy Brickify. It's a one-time purchase. No subscription, no monthly fee.
  3. Already bought it on this Apple ID or Google account? Use Restore Purchases instead.
  4. Note which account you bought it with. On Android, you'll sign in with that same account if you set up Device Owner mode later.

If the app carries straight on into setup, that's fine. Steps 6 to 9 walk through those screens one at a time, so read ahead if you'd rather do it in one sitting.
