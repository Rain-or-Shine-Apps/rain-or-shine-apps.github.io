The 12 days steps of Christmas · Step 6 of 12

Choose your PIN

“Kids watch thumbs.”

15 October 2026 · about 2 mins

← Back to Blog

Your Brickify PIN is the 4-digit code that unbricks the phone. Choose it like your child is watching. At some point, they will be.

Do it

  1. Pick 4 digits you'll remember, but won't enter on impulse.
  2. Not your phone unlock code. They've seen you type it a hundred times.
  3. Not a birthday, a year, 1234 or 0000.
  4. Enter it in Brickify when the setup asks.

Kids watch thumbs

Children learn a code from the pattern your thumb makes, not the numbers. Turn the phone away when you type it, every time, and especially in the first few weeks.

On iPhone you can reuse the same digits for the Screen Time passcode in Step 11, so there's only one code to remember.
