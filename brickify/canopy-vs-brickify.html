Canopy vs Brickify

Canopy watches what happens on the phone. Brickify doesn't. Here's the difference.

← Back to Information

The short version

Canopy is a parental-control suite on a subscription. It filters images and video as your child browses, looks for explicit photos, shows social media messages and flags worrying topics, and gives location alerts.

Brickify isn't a monitoring app. It doesn't read messages, browsing history or location. It decides which apps open, and when, on a schedule you set, for a one-off price.

Which one fits?

If you want to see what's in your child's messages, or filter what they see on the web, Canopy does that and Brickify doesn't.

If you'd rather limit which apps open and when, without reading their messages or tracking where they are, Brickify is the lighter option, and you pay once. More on that choice in what Brickify does, and doesn't.
