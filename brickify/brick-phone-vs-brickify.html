By the start of secondary school, most children already have a smartphone: ownership jumps from 56% at age 10 to 83% at age 11 (Ofcom, 2026). So the question isn't whether, but what's switched on, and when.

On the same side as the movement

Brickify isn't a rival to brick phones or to Smartphone Free Childhood. If you've signed the Pact and can wait until 14, wait. That's a good choice. Brickify is for families who've decided a phone is needed now, and for children who already have a smartphone that needs to be quieter. At 14, it helps too: instead of going from nothing to everything overnight, open one app at a time.
