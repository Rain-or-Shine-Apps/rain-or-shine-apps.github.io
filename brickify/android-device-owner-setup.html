Android setup — Device Owner mode

Brickify's real Android protection needs one extra, one-time step: a factory reset and a short guided setup. This page walks through it.

On Android phones Brickify has to be set up as the device owner so that it cannot be uninstalled. Here are the steps to set this up.

⚠️ Before you start: is this phone managed by an employer or school?

Device Owner mode and existing organisation management (a "Work profile," or IT department control) are mutually exclusive on Android — this setup will not work on a managed device, and attempting it on a fully-managed corporate phone will fail outright.

Check Settings for a "Work profile" section, or ask your IT department. If this phone is managed, use a personal, unmanaged device instead.

Setup

1

Confirm you've bought Brickify

If you tapped "Buy Brickify" from inside the app already, you're set — note which Google account you used, you'll need to sign into that same one later. If you haven't bought it yet, open Brickify on the phone first and follow the prompt there before continuing.

2

Open this page on a second device

You're about to erase this phone, so you won't have its screen available once the reset starts. Read through to the end here, on your own phone or a laptop, before you begin.

3

Factory reset the phone

Settings → System → Reset options → Erase all data (factory reset) — exact wording varies by phone manufacturer. This erases everything currently on it.

4

Reveal the QR scanner

On the blank "Welcome" screen that appears after the reset, tap the same spot on the screen 6 times in a row. A QR code scanner opens.

5

Scan this code

The phone needs internet access to download Brickify — connect to Wifi if prompted. It installs and sets itself up as Device Owner automatically, with no further taps needed.

You'll see screens mentioning "your organization" or "setting up a work device" — that's normal. This is generic Android language for this entire category of setup, used identically whether it's a corporate IT department or, in this case, you. There's no employer, school, or third party involved — it's just this phone's admin app, Brickify, which you're setting up. Once Brickify itself is installed, its details in Settings are labelled "Brickify," not "your organization," and explain exactly what it can and can't see.

6

Open Brickify

It's meant to open on its own once provisioning finishes, but doesn't always — if nothing happens within a minute or so, just open it manually from the app drawer like any other app.

7

Go through setup, then Restore Purchases

Brickify walks you through its own setup — a device PIN, installing any apps you want your child to have, adding your Google account at the system level (the same one you used to buy Brickify — signing into Play Store alone isn't enough), then Brickify's own passcode, second factor, recovery codes, and the rest. At the final "Ready to take control of your phone?" screen, tap Restore previous purchase instead of paying again — open the Play Store app once first if it doesn't find the purchase right away, to let it register the account.

Why the purchase carries over: your payment is tied to your Google account through Play Billing, not to the specific copy of the app installed on this exact phone. Restoring finds that same purchase and unlocks Brickify again — no second charge.

What happens in the Brickify setup wizard

Once the device-level steps above are done, Brickify walks you through its own setup — in this order, using the same names you'll see on screen:

1

Device PIN

A lock screen PIN for the phone itself — the one your child will know, to unlock and use the phone day to day. Required; QR setup skips Android's own lock-screen step.

2

Install apps

The phone's nearly empty after the wipe — install whatever you actually want your child to have from the Play Store before choosing what's allowed or blocked.

3

Google account

Add your Google account at the system level — the same one you bought Brickify with, so Restore Purchases can find it.

4

PIN

Brickify's own passcode — separate from the device PIN above, and this one's for you, not your child.

5

2nd factor

Parent PIN, authenticator app, or email code — backs up your passcode so losing one doesn't lock you out for good.

6

Recovery codes

Three one-time backup codes. Write them down — they won't be shown again.

7

Profile

Name it and choose which apps it blocks.

8

Notify email

Where Brickify emails you if something needs attention — the device goes silent, its protection gets removed, or (despite blocking) a new app appears.

9

Permissions

Enable the Accessibility Service — this is what actually blocks apps. Device Admin is already active automatically.

10

Unlock Brickify

Pay, or Restore Purchases if you already bought it — covered above.

Troubleshooting

  • Restore Purchases doesn't find anything — double check you're signed into the same Google account used to buy Brickify. Still stuck? Email brickify@rainorshineapps.com with your device ID (shown in Setup) and we'll sort it out.
  • QR scanner won't open — you need a genuinely blank, freshly-reset phone; it only appears on the very first "Welcome" screen, before any account is added.
  • Scan succeeds but nothing installs — the phone needs internet access to download Brickify. Connect to Wifi during the setup wizard if you weren't prompted automatically.
  • Settings shows a "Work profile" and this didn't work — see the warning above; Device Owner mode can't coexist with organisation-managed device settings.
