Troubleshooting

Fixes for the handful of things that occasionally come up during setup — most people won't hit any of these.

← Back to Help

Android

  • Restore Purchases doesn't find anything — double check you're signed into the same Google account used to buy Brickify. Still stuck? Email brickify@rainorshineapps.com with your device ID (shown in Setup) and we'll sort it out.
  • QR scanner won't open — you need a genuinely blank, freshly-reset phone; it only appears on the very first "Welcome" screen, before any account is added.
  • Scan succeeds but nothing installs — the phone needs internet access to download Brickify. Connect to Wifi during the setup wizard if you weren't prompted automatically.
  • Settings shows a "Work profile" and this didn't work — Device Owner mode can't coexist with organisation-managed device settings. See the warning on the Android setup guide.

iOS

  • Restore Purchases doesn't find anything — double check you're signed into the same Apple ID used to buy Brickify. Still stuck? Email brickify@rainorshineapps.com with your device ID (shown in Setup) and we'll sort it out.
  • Family Controls permission wasn't offered — it's asked for during the Profile step of setup; if you skipped it, go to Setup → Manage profiles and it'll be prompted again next time a profile needs saving.
  • Screen Time passcode not stopping app deletion — check Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions is turned on, and "Deleting Apps" is set to Don't Allow underneath it.
