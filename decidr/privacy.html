1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, including your name, email address, and profile photo. We also collect the decisions, factors, and options you create within the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our services, send you technical notices and support messages, respond to your comments and questions, and analyze usage patterns (if you've consented to analytics).

3. Analytics and Tracking

With your consent, we collect anonymous usage data to understand how our app is being used and to improve the user experience. You can opt out of analytics collection at any time in your profile settings.

4. Email Marketing and Notifications

If you opt in, we may send you marketing emails about community decisions, success stories, and product updates. You can manage these preferences at any time in your profile settings.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only when you choose to share decisions with specific users or make them public, with service providers who assist in operating our platform, or when required by law.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Your Rights and Data Retention

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time. You can manage your data through your profile settings or by contacting us directly. Upon deletion, all your personal data will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. This Website

This section covers our website, rainorshineapps.com, only — the app is covered by the rest of this policy. When you first visit, a banner asks whether we may use Google Analytics. If you choose Accept, Google Analytics sets cookies so we can count visits and see which pages and download buttons are used. It collects information such as the pages you view, your approximate location, and your device and browser type. We do not use it for advertising. If you choose No thanks, or ignore the banner, no analytics cookies are set and nothing is sent to Google. You can change your choice at any time using Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at rain.or.shine.apps@gmail.com
