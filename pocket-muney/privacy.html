1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when creating an account, including your name and email address. We also collect content you create within Pocket Muney, such as children's first names, chores, and chore completion records. We only store first names and chore data — never real financial information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve our services, send technical notices, and respond to your questions.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we never share it with advertisers or trackers. We may share it with service providers who help operate our platform, or when required by law.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access or disclosure.

5. Your Rights

You can access, update, or delete your personal information at any time through your profile settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Pocket Muney is a tool for parents to manage chores for their own children. The app is operated by an adult account holder; we do not knowingly collect information directly from children under 13. Only a child's first name and chore data are stored — no other personal information.

7. This Website

This section covers our website, rainorshineapps.com, only — the app is covered by the rest of this policy. When you first visit, a banner asks whether we may use Google Analytics. If you choose Accept, Google Analytics sets cookies so we can count visits and see which pages and download buttons are used. It collects information such as the pages you view, your approximate location, and your device and browser type. We do not use it for advertising. If you choose No thanks, or ignore the banner, no analytics cookies are set and nothing is sent to Google. You can change your choice at any time using Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by posting the new policy on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at rain.or.shine.apps@gmail.com
